# Language (English and Romanian)

She may speak to you in English or Romanian. Some clients write in Romanian, and their
file names may be in Romanian.

## Rules

- **Answer in the language she used** in her last message.
- **Draft replies in the client's language** (the language of the client's last email).
- **Keep fixed names in English:** stage names, folder names, Gmail labels, case Doc
  headings, document status words. This keeps case files consistent.
- **Case file log lines in English**, even when the email was in Romanian.
- **Search in both languages.** Gmail and Drive search match words, not meaning. For any
  search, try the English and the Romanian terms, with and without diacritics
  (`fluturaș` and `fluturas`).
